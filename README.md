# Adobe Premiere Pro – Product Management Case Study

This project is a product management case study focused on Adobe Premiere Pro.

## Project Overview

The project analyzes Adobe Premiere Pro from a product management perspective, covering its market position, target users, competitive landscape, and potential product strategies.

## Key Areas

- Product Life Cycle Analysis
- User Persona
- Value Proposition Canvas
- Competitive Analysis
- Competitor Mapping
- Product Strategy
- Retention Strategy
- AI Opportunities

## Competitive Landscape

The analysis includes competitors such as:

- DaVinci Resolve
- Final Cut Pro
- CapCut

It also considers AI-powered alternatives and emerging tools such as Runway and Pika.

## Product Strategy

The proposed strategy focuses on improving:

- User retention
- Editing speed and workflow efficiency
- AI-assisted editing capabilities
- Competitive differentiation
